Ta-Da Home Support

A real person reads every message.

Write to info@yourluni.com and say what happened and what you expected instead. If something looks wrong on screen, a screenshot saves us both a round trip.

My chores have disappeared

Check Rooms → Going away first: if the home is paused, nothing is due and the list looks empty. Tap I'm back to resume.

If they are really gone and you are signed in, the copy in your account usually still has them: sign in on the phone again, or reinstall and sign in. If you have a backup file instead, use Rooms → Backup & restore → Restore from a file. With neither, write to us before you do anything else — do not delete and reinstall the app, because that removes what is left.

How do I move to a new phone?

The easy way is to sign in on both. On the old phone, Rooms → Cloud backup, sign in, and let it back up. On the new phone, install Ta-Da Home and sign in with the same account: your home comes back with its history.

Without an account: on the old phone, Rooms → Backup & restore → Back up now, and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone. On the new phone choose Restore from a file.

Either way, photos are not included; they come across with your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup.

It asked which home to keep, and I picked the wrong one

When a phone that already has a home signs in to an account that also has one, the app asks which to keep, because the two cannot be added together — you would get two of every chore.

If you kept this phone's and wanted the account's, write to us: the account's old home is marked as gone rather than erased, so we can usually put it back. If you kept the account's and wanted the phone's, that one was only ever on the phone and we never had a copy — restore a backup file if you made one, and if not, tell us anyway and we will help you rebuild it.

Do I need an account?

No. Ta-Da Home works fully without one, and everything stays on your phone. An account exists so a lost, broken or reset phone does not lose your home. Signing in copies your rooms, chores, history, supplies and settings to your account — never your photos.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Rooms → your email address → Delete my account. Your account and the copy of your home in the cloud are deleted straight away and cannot be brought back. The home on that phone stays exactly as it is and keeps working. You can also ask us at info@yourluni.com.

I signed up and the confirmation email has not arrived

It can take a few minutes; check the spam folder first. The sign-in screen has a Send it again link while it waits. Signing in with Apple or Google needs no confirmation email at all.

Can I stop the cloud copy without losing anything?

Rooms → your email address → Sign out on this phone. The home on the phone is untouched and keeps working; the copy in your account is left alone too, ready for when you sign in again.

The daily reminder does not arrive

Check that notifications are allowed: iPhone Settings → Ta-Da Home → Notifications. Then in the app, Rooms → Daily reminder, pick a time again. There is no reminder on a day with nothing due — that is deliberate.

What does Ta-Da Pro include, and how do I cancel?

Pro adds the extras listed on the Pro screen. Cancel any time in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions; you keep Pro until the period you paid for ends.

Do my photos leave my phone?

No. Room and surface photos stay on the device. They are not uploaded to your account, and they are not in the backup file either. See the privacy policy.
