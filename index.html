Ta-Da Home Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 September 2026

The short version: Ta-Da Home works entirely on your iPhone and needs no account. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, ever. If you choose to switch on cloud backup, your home is copied to an account so a lost phone is not a lost home and the people you live with can share it — and even then, your photos never leave the phone.

Who we are

Ta-Da Home is made by Luni, Leliestraat 31, 2011 BL Haarlem, Netherlands (KvK 94418691). Questions about this policy go to info@yourluni.com.

Two ways to use the app

Without an account, nothing ever leaves your iPhone. The app makes no internet connections of its own, and we receive nothing at all.

With an account — which only exists if you make one — your home is stored on our behalf so that it survives a lost phone and can be shared with your household. Everything below says which of the two it is talking about.

What stays on your iPhone

Your rooms, chores and when you did them, your supplies, your settings and any photos you add are stored inside the app on your iPhone. Without an account we never receive them and cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Like any app's data, iOS may include it in your own iCloud or computer backup of the iPhone. Those backups are Apple's service, under Apple's privacy policy.

Camera and photos

The app asks for the camera or your photo library only when you tap a photo slot. The photo is saved inside the app on your iPhone and is never uploaded.

If you make an account

Signing in is optional and is for one thing: keeping your home somewhere it survives a lost phone, and letting the people you live with see it.

What we then hold: your email address; your name, if you give one or your sign-in provider supplies one; your rooms, chores and their settings; when each chore was done and by which member of your household; your supplies and their stock levels; your surfaces; and your household's own settings, such as the daily time and whether the home is paused.

What we never hold: your photos. They stay on the phone, are excluded from the backup file, and are not uploaded by sync. Nor do we hold a password — if you sign in with a password it is stored, hashed, by our hosting provider and is never visible to us; with Apple or Google there is no password at all.

Who else sees it: everyone in your household, because that is what sharing a home means. Nobody outside it. We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, and there is no advertising in the app.

Where it is: with Supabase, our hosting provider, on servers in Frankfurt, Germany, inside the EU. Supabase acts only on our instructions.

Why we are allowed to: because you asked us to provide the service — the lawful basis is the contract between us. You can withdraw at any time by deleting your account.

How long: until you delete it. There is no automatic expiry and no archive: deleted means gone.

Deleting your account

In the app: Rooms → Cloud backup & family → Delete my account. It removes your account and everything in it, and it takes effect immediately. If you were the last person in a household, the household goes too. The home on your phone is untouched and keeps working, with or without an account.

Reminders

The daily reminder is scheduled on your iPhone. No server is involved.

Backups and sharing you choose

When you tap Back up now, the app makes a file and opens the iOS share sheet. It goes only where you send it, such as Files, iCloud Drive or an email to yourself. When you share the week's plan, the text goes only to the person or app you pick. Either way it passes through the service you choose, not through us.

Subscriptions

Ta-Da Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your name, Apple ID or card. The app checks whether your subscription is active by asking the App Store on your iPhone. Apple's privacy policy covers purchases.

What Apple may share with us

If you have turned on Share with App Developers in your iPhone's Settings (Privacy & Security, then Analytics & Improvements), Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage totals. They do not identify you, and you can turn this off at any time. Apple also gives us anonymous sales totals.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly hold data about them. A household may of course contain children; if a child's name appears as a member of your household, it is there because an adult in that household typed it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you can ask what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct, export or delete it. Without an account we hold nothing, because the app sends us nothing; to erase what is on the phone, delete the app. With an account, the delete described above does it immediately, and you can write to info@yourluni.com for a copy of your data or with any complaint. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority — in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting something new, this page will say so before the update that does it is released. That is how this very section came to be written: version 1.0 of the app had no account at all.
